# Design directions — usaeventpartner.com

## Decision

**Selected: 1 + 3 — Corporate US × Network Edition.**

The production site combines the credibility and visual restraint of the Corporate US direction with the conversion architecture proven on merch.mt / corp-merch.eu.

### Production palette

- Navy `#10233F`
- Federal blue `#173E6D`
- Teal `#1C8C86`
- Soft mint `#DCECE8`
- Warm white `#F7F5EF`
- Steel `#DCE3EA`
- Signal red `#C84232`

### Production rules

- strong rectangular grid
- minimal radius, no pill UI
- oversized Helvetica/system-sans headlines
- thin rules and numbered modules
- real project photography as primary proof
- restrained signal-red accent rather than flag-themed decoration
- same high-conversion rhythm as the existing network: hero → services → work → benefits → special product → urgency → process → FAQ → brief

### Hero

Eyebrow: `US EVENT PRODUCTION FOR INTERNATIONAL TEAMS`

H1: `You bring the event. We handle the US.`

Primary CTA: `Send Your Brief`

Secondary CTA: `Explore Services`

### Why 1+3

The result looks like a credible US B2B event-production company while staying operationally cheap to maintain. We reuse the tested content, lead-form, SEO, analytics and Cloudflare patterns from the previous sites, but the visual identity and commercial offer are independent.

## Archived direction 1 — Corporate US

Strict modern US B2B. Navy / federal blue / warm white / signal red. Strong grid, large project photography, rectangular CTAs and almost no decorative UI.

## Archived direction 2 — American Heritage

Slightly conservative operator-led direction. Deep ink, heritage navy, parchment and brass; selected serif typography for a premium service-brochure feel.

## Archived direction 3 — Network Edition

Closest structural sibling to merch.mt / corp-merch.eu. Midnight + teal + warm white + orange accent with the same established section rhythm.
